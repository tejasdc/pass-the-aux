Pass the Aux / Suprematist / motion studies

A painting with a downbeat.

Same gallery cream and flat pigments. Three different ways to make a beat visible from across the table. Arrows describe motion, not interface elements.

01 / Split square

A black square fractures into four heavy planes on each beat. A vermilion beam passes through the opening; cobalt answers below. Slow eight-beat tipping changes the balance.

Strength: one memorable silhouette; the opening is legible even on a small phone.

02 / Eclipse crossing

A dark disc with two colored beams orbiting past it. On the beat the disc grows and the beams cross.

Tradeoff: readable, but resembles a record or planetary diagram. Rotation overwhelms the percussive signal.

03 / Flight score

Parallel colored planes launch and retreat in a four-beat relay. Energy controls the distance between them.

Tradeoff: close to Malevich's flying planes, but reads like a tilted equalizer and duplicates the page's scattered bars.

Selection: split square. For this small party display it offers the strongest beat gesture and the clearest distinction from the surrounding floating shapes. The existing drifting composition preserves familiarity but does not meet the request for a new idea.
